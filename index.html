About

Hi, I'm Chava.

I'm Salvador “Chava” Márquez, a photographer and videographer based in Reno, Nevada, with more than ten years behind the camera. I shoot weddings, couples, maternity, milestones and creative studio work across Reno, Lake Tahoe and wherever the story takes me.

My sessions are calm and guided. I'll tell you where to stand and what to do with your hands, then step back and catch what happens naturally. I work in English and Spanish, and I'm just as comfortable leading on photo or on film.

I'm also a co-founder of Lake & Pine Collective, and I'm currently building my own studio space in Reno.
